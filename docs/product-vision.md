# Product Vision — Mithila Web Labs Website

_Status: Phase 0 draft, awaiting approval · Last updated: 2026-10-02_

## 1. Product vision

The Mithila Web Labs website is the company's main sales asset. It needs to do three things:

1. **Show the company is credible.** A business owner landing on the site should see a real engineering company, not a freelancer template.
2. **Show what we can build.** The site itself (its speed, polish, and AI demo) and the portfolio show that we can build working AI-powered business software.
3. **Turn interest into qualified enquiries.** Every page leads to one action: describe your business problem.

Success test: within 10 seconds a visitor should be able to say, *"These people build software and AI tools that fix business processes like mine."*

### What success looks like (first 6 months)
| Signal | Target |
|---|---|
| Qualified enquiries | First 3–5 real conversations |
| AI demo → contact conversion | Track it; establish a baseline |
| Core Web Vitals | All "Good" (LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1) |
| Running cost | ≈ ₹0–2,000/month (see roadmap.md, cost section) |

## 2. Target users

| Persona | Context | What they need to see |
|---|---|---|
| **Indian SME founder / MD** (primary) | 10–200 staff. Runs on Excel, WhatsApp, Tally, and email. Cost-conscious. Has been burned by agencies before. | Plain language, problems they recognise, evidence we deliver, an easy way to talk to us (including WhatsApp) |
| **Operations head** | Owns the painful process: approvals, data entry, status chasing | A clear "before → after" workflow and what changes for their team |
| **CTO / tech lead** (growing company) | Judges engineering quality | Architecture, stack, security posture, and code quality signals in the portfolio |
| **Product manager** | Wants a build partner | Process: discovery → prototype → iterate |
| **UAE/GCC business owner** (secondary, later) | Higher budgets, expects international polish | Premium design, English-first, no "cheap offshore" signals |

## 3. Value proposition

**Headline:** AI-Powered Business Applications & Digital Products

**One-liner:** We turn business problems into practical digital products, intelligent workflows, and AI-powered applications.

**Why us (proof points we can actually claim today):**
- **We start from the business process.** We map how work actually happens before proposing software.
- **We build working prototypes quickly.** You see a working prototype early, not a slide deck.
- **We use AI where it helps.** It goes where it removes manual work, not everywhere.
- **We build production-quality software.** That means security, testing, and maintainability, the same standards shown in our own projects.
- **One accountable team.** You talk directly to the people who build the software.

**What we deliberately do NOT claim (yet):** client logos, testimonials, years in business, team size, or ROI numbers. We will add these only when they are real.
